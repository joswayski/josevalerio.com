# Places 3D asset sources

The vegetation models in `public/places/models/nature/` are by
[Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/) and were downloaded from
[Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/u/Quaternius).

They are released into the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
